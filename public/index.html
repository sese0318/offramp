Your taste, on sleep’s side.

Feeds know exactly what you love, and that is why they keep you up. Offramp uses the same kind of knowledge to walk you down. It takes what you love from the Qloo taste graph, puts it in a calming order with NVIDIA Nemotron, and gives the night an ending.

    Why taste, why order

    Generic sleep advice says read a book or listen to calm music. Which book, which music? Advice that is not yours is easy to ignore, and the feed is never generic. Offramp keeps the part of the feed that works (it knows you) and drops the part that hurts (it never ends, and it gets more exciting).

    • Binge viewing was linked to poorer sleep, more fatigue and more insomnia symptoms in 423 young adults, and cognitive pre-sleep arousal fully mediated the link. Exelmans and Van den Bulck, J Clin Sleep Med, 2017. doi 10.5664/jcsm.6704
    • Bedtime procrastination, going to bed later than intended with nothing external stopping you, is linked to insufficient sleep. Kroese et al., Front Psychol, 2014. doi 10.3389/fpsyg.2014.00611
    • The if-you-are-still-awake step follows stimulus control, a standard part of CBT-I.

    Private by design

    The server keeps nothing. The agent state travels with each request and is signed so it cannot be edited in the browser. Your morning log lives only in this browser’s storage and is sent to the model only when you press Reflect.

    Every step needs an entity id from a Qloo response in the same run, and the step text is scanned for titles Qloo did not return. A draft that fails goes back to the planner with the reasons. When a Tavily key is set, one more step searches the web for a single calm episode of the first show or podcast, and keeps the pick only if its title appears in the search results.

    Offramp is a wellness tool, not medical care. If trouble sleeping lasts for months, talk to a clinician about cognitive behavioral therapy for insomnia (CBT-I).
